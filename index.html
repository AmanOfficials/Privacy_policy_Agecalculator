Privacy Policy

App: Age Calculator | Developer: Amanullah | Last updated: September 2026

Summary: The Age Calculator app operates primarily as an offline utility. We do not sell, store, or harvest any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, date of birth, or calculation history on external servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Age Calculator is designed with user privacy at its core. When you enter dates or calculate intervals:

  • Date of Birth & Calculation Data: All calculations are processed locally in real time entirely within your device's active memory. This information is never uploaded, tracked, or transmitted anywhere.
  • Personal Identifiers: We do not require account creation, logins, phone numbers, or access to your contacts.

2. Technical Information & Third-Party Services

To ensure app performance, stability, and delivery via the Google Play Store, the application may rely on standard framework services provided by Google Play Services:

  • Crash Reports & Performance: In the event of an app malfunction, anonymous diagnostic logs (such as device OS version and crash trace data) may be collected to help diagnose technical bugs.
  • Advertisements (if enabled): If Google AdMob or an ad provider is present in the build, anonymous advertising IDs may be used strictly in accordance with Google's Play Console Developer Policies to serve relevant non-personalized ads.

3. Device Permissions

The app does not request or require intrusive permissions (such as GPS location, camera, audio, or internal file storage). Any system permissions used are limited strictly to the fundamental requirements for APK rendering and standard network availability checks.

4. Children’s Privacy

Age Calculator complies with the requirements of the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any minor data has inadvertently reached us, please reach out so it can be promptly purged.

5. Security

Because zero sensitive user data is collected or maintained on our own servers, the risk of data exposure or breaches regarding your personal dates and calculations is effectively eliminated.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory compliance. Any revisions will be published directly to this page with an updated revision date.
